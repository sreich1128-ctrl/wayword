- **match:** a native wrote exactly our draft.
- **close:** at least 80% the same (often punctuation, an article, or word order).
- **differs:** natives wrote it differently. Not necessarily wrong (many sentences have several correct forms), but compare.
- **none:** no native translation of that exact English exists on Tatoeba.

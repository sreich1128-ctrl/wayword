## What's wrong, in three patterns

1. **Padding.** Most situations list 15–20 general phrases (hello, thank you, can I pay by card) to look full. They belong in The basics and a shared "Ordering & paying" set, taught once. A situation should show its own phrases first and mention the general ones as "you already know these".
2. **Two social topics that are really one.** Flirting is Meeting People with three extra lines.
3. **Missing situations a tourist actually meets.** No airport, no pharmacy/health beyond "Where is the pharmacy?", no sightseeing/tickets, no bar. Numbers, times and prices (understanding what you're told) aren't taught anywhere.

## Proposed fixes for Level 1

Each new phrase is written in 9 languages as a labelled draft (`content/added/`), so the list is deliberately short (principle 1: useful over complete).

**Flirting: rebuild (keep 3, add ~9, stop sharing with Meeting People)**
- Keep: You're funny. · I like talking to you. · Can I get your Instagram?
- Add: Can I buy you a drink? · Would you like to get a coffee? · You have a beautiful smile. · I like your style. · Are you here with friends? · Are you free tomorrow? · I'd like to see you again. · Can I have your number? · I had a great time.
- Add the exits (useful, and a safety matter): No thank you, I'm not interested. · I have a boyfriend / girlfriend. · Please leave me alone.
- Replies to understand: Maybe. · I'd love to. · I have a boyfriend / girlfriend. · Sorry, I can't.

**Meeting People: make it its own**
- Add: My name is ___. · I'm from ___. · Nice to meet you. · How long are you here? · What do you recommend seeing here? · Cheers!
- Swap "Have you ever been to the United States?" → "Have you been to ___?" (a frame).
- Move "I'm learning by trying to speak" here from Flirting.
- Replies: Where are you from? · How long are you staying? · Do you like it here?

**Help & emergencies: fill the gaps**
- Add: Call an ambulance. · Where is the hospital? · It hurts here. · I've been robbed. · I need medicine for ___. (frame: pain, fever, allergy, stomach)
- Replies: What happened? · Where does it hurt? · Do you have insurance? · Wait here.

**New situations (each ~8 own phrases + 3 replies)**
- **Airport:** Where is check-in? · Where is gate ___? · My luggage didn't arrive. · Is the flight delayed? · I have a connecting flight. · Where is baggage claim? · A window / aisle seat, please. · Replies: Passport, please. · Anything to declare? · Boarding has started.
- **Sightseeing:** Two tickets, please. · What time does it open / close? · Is there a discount for students? · Can I take photos? · Where is the entrance? · Is there a guided tour in English? · Replies: It's closed today. · It's sold out. · Follow me.
- **Bar:** A beer, please. · A glass of red / white wine. · What do you have on tap? · Another round. · Replies: What can I get you? · Last orders.
- **Pharmacy** could be folded into Help & emergencies via the "I need medicine for ___" frame rather than a new situation.

**Padding: no content change, a display change**
- Each situation lists its own phrases; shared ones collapse under "You already know these (n)". Readiness already counts mostly own phrases (`counts`); extend that to every situation.

**Numbers, times, prices:** a small "Numbers" set (1–10, 20, 50, 100; "at three o'clock"; "five euros") as listening practice, because these are what you get told back.

Rough size: ~45 new phrases and ~20 replies × 9 languages (drafts), then recordings for Portuguese if wanted.

## Levels (owner's decision 2026-11-14)

- **Level 1 · Basic** (about A1): today's content, after this audit's fixes.
- **Level 2 · Communicative** (about A2): the same situations again, with longer sentences, follow-up questions and more to understand. All languages, needed for publication.
- **Level 3 · Conversational** (about B1): answering the unexpected. Call and response with the mic, built up with tiles first (pick the sentence → build it → say part → say all). Piloted in **Spanish** only.
- Certificate wording: "Wayword Level N", never "A2/B1 certificate". The teaching method stays as it is (it already follows the Pimsleur-style loop: prompt, answer aloud, growing gaps, fading support).
- Finishing a level unlocks the next; a quiz lets you jump ahead; earlier levels stay open for review.
